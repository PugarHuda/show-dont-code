Analyse your own lab video

An MP4 or MOV up to 5 minutes, filmed from your point of view while you pipette. It takes about 5 to 8 minutes: Muse Spark watches it, SAM 3.1 tracks the pipette, Muse Voice Transcribe listens, and a Muse Spark Open agent checks the result in the Opentrons simulator. Uploaded videos are stored for this demo and only people with the link can open the result.
